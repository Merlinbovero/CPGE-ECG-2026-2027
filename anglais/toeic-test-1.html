Test blanc TOEIC n°1 — Reading

Un entraînement complet au format TOEIC Reading (Parties 5, 6 et 7), soit 62 questions. Note tes réponses sur une feuille, puis ouvre le corrigé à la fin. Temps conseillé : ≈ 50 minutes (rythme d'examen).

⚠️ Contenu original, pas une annale officielle

Le TOEIC est un test propriétaire d'ETS : ses vrais sujets ne sont pas librement diffusables. Ce test blanc est entièrement original, rédigé pour reproduire le format et le niveau officiels — pas pour copier un examen réel. Pour des exemples officiels, voir les ressources légales.

Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences (Q1–30)

Choisis le mot ou l'expression qui complète le mieux la phrase.

1. The marketing team will meet ____ Monday to discuss the new campaign.
(A) in(B) on(C) at(D) by
2. All employees must submit their expense reports ____ the end of the month.
(A) by(B) until(C) since(D) for
3. The manager asked the interns to work more ____.
(A) efficient(B) efficiency(C) efficiently(D) efficiencies
4. Our new software is much ____ than the previous version.
(A) fast(B) faster(C) fastest(D) more fast
5. The shipment ____ yesterday, so it should arrive tomorrow.
(A) sends(B) sent(C) was sent(D) is sending
6. Ms. Tanaka is responsible ____ training new staff.
(A) of(B) for(C) to(D) with
7. If the client ____ the contract, we will begin next week.
(A) sign(B) signs(C) signed(D) will sign
8. The conference room is ____ occupied; please use room B.
(A) current(B) currently(C) currency(D) currents
9. Neither the director ____ the assistant was available for comment.
(A) or(B) nor(C) and(D) but
10. Employees are encouraged to ____ advantage of the training programs.
(A) make(B) do(C) take(D) have
11. The report must be reviewed ____ it is published.
(A) before(B) during(C) despite(D) although
12. Sales have increased ____ 15 percent this quarter.
(A) at(B) by(C) on(D) of
13. The company ____ its headquarters to Lyon last year.
(A) moves(B) moved(C) has moved(D) moving
14. Please make sure that all documents are ____ signed.
(A) proper(B) properly(C) property(D) properties
15. ____ the bad weather, the delivery arrived on time.
(A) Because of(B) Despite(C) However(D) So
16. The new policy applies to ____ who works in the finance department.
(A) everyone(B) someone(C) no one(D) anywhere
17. We are looking forward to ____ from you soon.
(A) hear(B) hearing(C) heard(D) hears
18. The technician will ____ the equipment before the demonstration.
(A) inspect(B) inspection(C) inspecting(D) inspected
19. This special offer is valid ____ December 31.
(A) since(B) until(C) by(D) from
20. The proposal was rejected because it was too ____.
(A) expense(B) expensively(C) expensive(D) expenses
21. ____ you need any assistance, please contact the front desk.
(A) Should(B) Would(C) Could(D) May
22. The results of the survey were ____ than expected.
(A) good(B) well(C) better(D) best
23. All visitors are required to ____ in at reception.
(A) sign(B) signing(C) signed(D) signs
24. During busy periods, the factory operates ____ full capacity.
(A) at(B) in(C) on(D) to
25. The CEO's speech was both informative ____ inspiring.
(A) or(B) but(C) and(D) so
26. We regret to inform you that your application ____ successful.
(A) is not(B) was not(C) has not(D) not was
27. The department needs to hire someone with ____ experience in accounting.
(A) extend(B) extensive(C) extensively(D) extension
28. Employees ____ have completed the safety course may operate the machines.
(A) who(B) which(C) whose(D) whom
29. The meeting has been ____ until further notice.
(A) postpone(B) postponed(C) postponing(D) postpones
30. Please reply ____ this email to confirm your attendance.
(A) at(B) to(C) on(D) for

Part 6 — Text Completion (Q31–46)

Complète chaque texte ; certaines questions demandent d'insérer une phrase entière.

Questions 31–34 — Email

Dear Mr. Chen,
Thank you for your interest in our services. We (31)____ your request and will send a detailed quote by Friday. (32)____, please let us know if you have any special requirements. Our team is (33)____ to help you at any time. (34)____ We look forward to working with you.
Best regards,
Anna Roux, Client Services

31.
(A) received(B) have received(C) receiving(D) receives
32.
(A) In the meantime(B) Therefore(C) However(D) As a result
33.
(A) able(B) available(C) possible(D) capable
34.
(A) We apologize for the delay.(B) Do not hesitate to contact us with any questions.(C) The store will be closed on Monday.(D) Payment is due upon receipt.

Questions 43–46 — Memo

To all staff:
Starting next month, the company (43)____ a new expense reporting system. The new system is designed to make the process faster and more (44)____. Training sessions (45)____ held during the first week of the month. (46)____ Please make sure to attend one of these sessions. Thank you.

43.
(A) introduces(B) will introduce(C) introduced(D) has introduced
44.

Part 7 — Reading Comprehension (Q47–62)

Lis chaque document et réponds aux questions.

Questions 47–49 — Email

From: Laura Mendes, Facilities Manager   To: All Staff   Subject: Parking changes

Dear colleagues,
Due to construction work in the main parking lot, the north entrance will be closed from March 3 to March 14. During this time, please use the south entrance and park in Lot C. A free shuttle will run every 15 minutes between Lot C and the main building from 7:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m. and from 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. We apologize for any inconvenience and thank you for your patience. If you have questions, contact the facilities office at extension 245.

47. Why was the email sent?
(A) To announce a new hire(B) To inform staff about parking changes(C) To schedule a meeting(D) To request expense reports
48. How often will the shuttle run?
(A) Every 15 minutes(B) Every 30 minutes(C) Every hour(D) Twice a day
49. What should staff do if they have questions?
(A) Visit Lot C(B) Email the CEO(C) Call the facilities office(D) Wait until March 14

GreenLeaf Café is seeking a part-time barista for our downtown location. Candidates should have at least one year of experience in customer service and be available to work weekends. Questions 53–55 — Article

Remote Work on the Rise
A recent study by the Institute for Workplace Research found that the number of employees working from home has doubled over the past five years. According to the report, 42 percent of office workers now spend at least two days per week working remotely. Companies say the main benefits are lower office costs and higher employee satisfaction. However, some managers worry that remote work may reduce collaboration between team members. To address this, many firms now organize regular in-person meetings and team-building events.

53. What is the article mainly about?
(A) The growth of remote work(B) A new office building(C) A company's financial results(D) A drop in salaries
54. What percentage of office workers work remotely at least two days a week?
(A) 15%(B) 42%(C) 50%(D) 84%
55. What concern do some managers have?
(A) Higher office costs(B) Reduced collaboration(C) Lower salaries(D) Longer commutes

Questions 58–62 — Double passage (email & reply)

Email 1 — From: Daniel Ortega, Meridian Consulting   To: Sunrise Hotel Reservations   Subject: Booking inquiry
Hello, I would like to book a conference room for a business meeting on June 18 for approximately 20 people. We will need a projector and coffee service for the morning. Could you also tell me whether parking is available for our guests? Thank you, Daniel Ortega.

Email 2 — From: Sunrise Hotel Events Team   To: Daniel Ortega   Subject: RE: Booking inquiry
Dear Mr. Ortega, Thank you for your inquiry. We are pleased to confirm that our Lakeside Room is available on June 18. It can accommodate up to 25 people and includes a projector at no extra charge. Coffee service can be arranged for €8 per person. Free parking is available for all guests in our underground garage. To confirm your reservation, please reply by June 4. We look forward to welcoming you.

58. Why did Mr. Ortega write the first email?
(A) To cancel a reservation(B) To inquire about booking a conference room(C) To complain about a service(D) To apply for a job
59. In addition to a projector and coffee, what does Mr. Ortega ask about?
(A) Restaurant menus(B) Parking availability(C) Flight schedules(D) Room decoration
60. What is included at no extra charge?
(A) Coffee service(B) The projector(C) Lunch(D) A guided tour
61. How much does coffee service cost?
(A) It is free(B) €8 per person(C) €18 per person(D) €25 per room
62. By when must Mr. Ortega confirm the reservation?
(A) June 4(B) June 18(C) July 4(D) May 20
